PERSONAL PROJECT / EMBEDDED SYSTEMS

Flower Tokens — Connected Keepsakes

I designed and built a pair of handheld devices that send little signals to each other over Wi-Fi. Each token uses an ESP32-C3, addressable LEDs, and a vibration motor. I also designed and 3D-printed the enclosures, painted them, soldered the electronics, and tested the finished devices.

ECE 112 / MECHATRONICS

DC Motor Encoder & Position Logging

I wired an Arduino Mega motor-and-encoder setup with an LCD display, then connected it to MATLAB to record shaft position over time. The main challenge was getting the serial communication and data capture working reliably. The final recording compared a fixed reference angle with encoder measurements as I turned the shaft by hand.

LUNARBOTICS / ROBOTICS OUTREACH

Servo-Driven Robotic Arm

As part of a Lunarbotics outreach project, I worked on a multi-joint robotic arm with 3D-printed components and servo motors. My work has included Arduino Mega control, servo positioning, and getting commands from a computer to the hardware over serial communication.
