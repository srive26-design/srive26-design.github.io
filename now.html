Proportional motor control

I'm working with a gearmotor, a quadrature encoder, and an Arduino Mega to understand the difference between commanding an angle and actually reaching it.

What I'm doing

  • Recording angle measurements in MATLAB and comparing them against a reference.
  • Trying different proportional gains and looking at the response rather than guessing what a "good" gain should be.
  • Debugging serial capture and interpreting what the encoder is telling me.

Next documentation step

Export a clean comparison figure showing the responses for several tested gains, including axes and the reference line.

Audio and room acoustics in MATLAB

In Signals & Systems coursework, I recorded voice samples and simulated how different impulse responses change what a sound recording feels like. I compared environments with clearer and more echo-like behavior.

I'd like to turn the plots and WAV outputs into a small interactive explanation of convolution and room response once the files are organized.

I'm also spending time learning how to analyze transistors, design circuits, and connect the equations from class to what happens on physical hardware.
